01Who we are

AutoAura (“AutoAura”, “we”, “us”) is a service for car repair workshops to find and ask for car spare parts, lubricants and related services. The AutoAura app is operated by Baaz Car Services, LS-1, Kanpur - Lucknow Rd, Sector E, Sector C1, LDA Colony, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226012, India.

This policy explains what information we collect when you use the AutoAura mobile app and related services (together, the “Service”), and how we use and protect it. By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Mobile number: used to log in. We verify it with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Workshop details: workshop name, owner name, workshop address (address line, city, state, pincode).
  • Vehicles: the cars you are looking for parts for (brand, model, year and fuel type).
  • Parts queries: the parts you select, your written parts lists, notes, and the photos you add (for example photos of a part, damage or a handwritten list).
  • Messages with us: when you call us, message us on WhatsApp or email us, we receive what you send through that channel.

Information collected automatically

  • Location (only if you allow it): when you tap “use my location” while entering your workshop address, we use your phone’s location once to fill in the address, and store the coordinates with the address.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, your phone gives the app a push token so we can send you updates on your queries.
  • Usage and technical data: dates and times you log in and use the Service, and standard server logs (such as IP address, device type and app version) that our hosting provider records to keep the Service running and secure.
We do not collect payment card or bank details in the app, we do not access your contacts, and we do not track your location in the background.

04How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in with OTP.
  • To show parts that suit your vehicle, receive your parts queries, prepare prices and availability, and contact you about them.
  • To send you notifications about replies and status changes on your queries.
  • To deliver parts or arrange services you confirm with us, using your workshop address and phone number.
  • To provide support, answer your messages and resolve disputes.
  • To keep the Service safe: prevent fraud and misuse, and block accounts that break our terms.
  • To improve the Service, for example by understanding which categories and parts are asked for most.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax, accounting and lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

06Storage and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our servers, database and admin panel is limited to authorised team members with their own logins. Passwords for our systems and secret keys are kept in secure secret storage, not in the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any incident.

07How long we keep it

  • Account and workshop details: while your account is active, and deleted or anonymised after you ask us to delete your account, unless we must keep them longer by law.
  • Queries, photos and quotes: kept with your account so you and our team can refer back to them; deleted with your account, except records we must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons.
  • Notification tokens: removed when you log out, or when the token stops working.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

08Your rights and choices

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update it: you can edit your workshop details in the app under Profile, or ask us.
  • Delete your account and personal information: email us from the contact details below, mentioning your registered mobile number.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by turning off camera, location or notification permissions, or by deleting your account.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (see below).

We will respond to requests within a reasonable time and as required by law. We may need to verify your identity first.

09Children

The Service is meant for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by other suitable means. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
