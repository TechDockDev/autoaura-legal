Delete your account

How to ask AutoAura to delete your workshop account and its data, what we delete, what we must keep, and how long it takes.

01How to ask

Send us a deletion request from any of these. You don't need the app installed.

WayWhat to send
EmailWrite to baazcarservices@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my AutoAura account”, your registered mobile number and your workshop name.
WhatsAppMessage +91 91188 84662 from your registered number: “Please delete my AutoAura account.”
PhoneCall +91 91188 84662 from your registered number and ask for account deletion.
In the appOpen Profile → Need help? → WhatsApp and ask for account deletion.
Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Send a request as above.

02How we check it's you

To stop anyone else deleting your account, we confirm the request comes from the account's owner. If you write from the registered number (WhatsApp or call), that is usually enough. If you email, we may call or message the registered number to confirm before we delete anything.

03What we delete

  • Your account and login (mobile number) and your login code.
  • Workshop details: workshop name, owner name, address and saved location.
  • Your saved vehicles.
  • Your parts queries, custom quote requests, notes and the photos you added, and our replies to them.
  • Notification tokens for your phones, so you stop getting notifications.

04What we keep

We keep only what the law requires, and only for as long as it requires:

  • Order and invoice records for parts you bought from us, kept for tax and accounting law (up to 8 years under Indian law), with only the details those records need.
  • Security and server logs, which are deleted automatically within 90 days.
  • A record that we deleted your account at your request (date and the last 4 digits of your number), so we can show we did it.

Database backups that still contain your data are overwritten in their normal backup cycle and are never used to restore a deleted account.

05How long it takes

We confirm we got your request within 2 working days and delete your account and data within 30 days. We tell you when it's done. After that the account can't be restored. You can sign up again later with the same number, as a new account.

06Delete some data only

You can keep your account and ask us to delete only some data, for example a query's photos or a vehicle. Ask in the same ways as above and say what to delete. You can also remove vehicles yourself in the app under Profile.
