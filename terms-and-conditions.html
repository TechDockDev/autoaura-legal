02What AutoAura does

AutoAura helps car repair workshops find and ask for car spare parts, lubricants and related services. Through the app you can:

  • add the vehicles you work on and browse parts by category;
  • send a parts query for selected parts, or a custom quote request with a written list or photos;

Sending a query is a request for a quote, not an order. There is no payment in the app; nothing is charged when you send a query.
